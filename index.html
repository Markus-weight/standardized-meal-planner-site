Standardized Meal Planner
IN DEVELOPMENT

Structured nutrition planning with less daily effort.

Standardized Meal Planner calculates individualized meal portions and nutritional values, supports repeatable meal planning and prepares selected nutrition data for transfer to health platforms.

FocusDiet & Nutrition
LanguagesGerman · English planned
PlatformAndroid
Health integrationHealth Connect · Samsung Health
CORE FEATURES

A planning system designed for consistency and flexibility.

Individualized portions

Meal quantities and nutritional values can be adapted to user profiles, goals and household size.

Repeatable meal planning

A structured recipe system reduces daily decision effort while recipes and ingredients remain editable.

Nutrition data transfer

Selected nutrition records can be transferred by the user to supported health platforms such as Health Connect and Samsung Health.

Multilingual foundation

The product is being prepared for German and English use with language-independent nutrition data.

PURPOSE

General wellness and nutrition planning.

Standardized Meal Planner is intended for general fitness, wellness and nutrition planning. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment and is not intended to replace professional medical advice.

CURRENT STATUS

Product development and platform integration are underway.

  • Android application under active development
  • Health Connect nutrition transfer implemented
  • Samsung Health Data SDK integration in development
  • English-language version planned
